Say what you ate. Check it. Done.

No scrolling through menus of “Dal, yellow, cooked, with oil”. Speak or type a sentence, take a photo of your food, or scan a package label, and openKCal drafts the entries for you.

  1. Tap the microphone and say it, snap a food picture, or scan a label.
  2. openKCal finds each food or creates a new shared recipe dish with ingredients.
  3. You review the items before anything is saved: adjust portions, 1-tap accept and log.

What it does

Made for kitchens where nothing comes with a barcode, and still happy to scan the things that do.

AI & Logging Skills

  • Photo to Recipe DishClick a photo of your meal and openKCal identifies dishes & ingredients, ready to log or save as a community recipe dish in 1 tap.
  • Nutrition Label OCRSnap a photo of the nutrition facts label (+ optional product front) on branded foods to parse macros automatically into the database.
  • Voice & Natural LanguageA sentence becomes diary entries and dishes you confirm.
  • Shared Community FoodsEvery custom food and recipe dish created by any user is immediately available across the community library.
  • Indian Food DatabaseOfficial composition tables (IFCT 2017) + 5,431 cooked dishes with household portions.

openGym Live Sync

  • Pairing Code RedemptionRedeem a 6-character openGym pairing code in 1 tap to link your training and nutrition seamlessly.
  • Workout Calorie BurnsAutomatically imports openGym workout sets and calculates active MET-based calorie expenditures.
  • Weigh-In SynchronizationSyncs scale weights and body measurements directly to progress graphs and dynamic TDEE calculations.
  • Lightweight Revision PollingZero-bandwidth background polling ensures data stays up to date without draining battery.

Privacy & Self-Hosting

  • Works Offline on PhoneThe Android app keeps everything on your phone with no account required.
  • Or on Your Own ServerSync your phone, tablet, and laptop with end-to-end device management.
  • Your AI, Your KeyConnect Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, or local Ollama/LM Studio with personal keys sealed on your server.
  • Passkeys & FingerprintsSign in with your biometric passkey, device code, or password.
  • Full Data OwnershipExport and import your complete diary, foods, and weights anytime in CSV or JSON.
