Say what you ate. Check it. Done.

No scrolling through menus of “Dal, yellow, cooked, with oil”. Speak or type a sentence and Thally drafts the entries for you.

  1. Tap the microphone and say it: “two rotis, a katori of dal and a chai”.
  2. Thally finds each food among yours and the 5,956 it knows, and estimates the grams. A home-made dish it hasn’t seen before gets drafted from its ingredients.
  3. You see every item before anything is saved. Fix the grams, move it to another meal, untick what is wrong, then log it.

What it does

Made for kitchens where nothing comes with a barcode, and still happy to scan the things that do.

Logging

  • By voice or textA sentence becomes diary entries you confirm.
  • Indian food first525 foods from India’s official composition tables, plus 5,431 cooked dishes with cup, piece and slice portions.
  • Your own dishesJoin ingredients into your rajma chawal, say how many it serves, weigh the pot if you like. It shows up first in every search.
  • BarcodesScan a packet, or type its number, and it is looked up on Open Food Facts.
  • A photo of your plateGet a first draft from a picture, then correct it.

Your numbers

  • Full nutrition for every foodEnergy, protein, carbs and sugars, fat and saturated fat, fibre and sodium, for what you ate and per 100 g.
  • Daily targetsWorked out from your height, weight, activity and goal.
  • Weight and waterA trend line towards your goal weight, and glasses of water.
  • A weekly reviewA short read of your week against your targets, if you want one.
  • Meal remindersA nudge at lunch, skipped when lunch is already logged.

Your data, your people

  • Works on its ownThe Android app keeps everything on your phone. No account needed.
  • Or on your own serverSync your phone and laptop, and invite family with a code.
  • Your AI, your keyVoice and photo logging use the AI you connect: Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, or a model on your own computer.
  • PasskeysSign in with your fingerprint or face, or a password.
  • Take it with youExport everything as a backup file or a spreadsheet, any time.
